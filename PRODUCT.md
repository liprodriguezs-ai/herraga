## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 7 (static output, `astro build` → `dist/`). Deploy target undecided; the build must run on any static host (Cloudflare, Netlify, cPanel).

## Users

Fleet owners, transport-company buyers, diesel mechanics and workshop owners in Mexico who need a specific part for a medium/heavy-duty diesel engine (cargo and passenger transport). They usually arrive knowing the engine (e.g. Cummins ISX, Detroit Serie 60 DDEC 4) and want to confirm availability and get a quote fast, often from a phone in a workshop.

## Product Purpose

HERRAGA S. de R.L. de C.V. (HERRAGA Refacciones) sells original, warrantied replacement parts for medium and heavy-duty diesel engines. The landing must let a buyer confirm "they have parts for my engine" and start a quote on WhatsApp within seconds.

## Positioning

Specialists in medias reparaciones (in-frame overhaul kits) and cabezas de cilindro, with per-engine coverage across Cummins, Detroit, Volvo, Mercedes-Benz, Navistar, Ford-Navistar, Caterpillar and Hino; five branches (CDMX, Cuautitlán Izcalli, Huehuetoca, Querétaro, Tijuana) plus shipping to all of Mexico.

## Operating Context

Sales happen by phone/WhatsApp with a specialized advisor. Payment: card, bank transfer, payment link, cash. National line: 664 514 9674 (WhatsApp).
